# Next Shift
 
Next Shift is a handoff tool for home health nurses. You leave report for the next nurse and it gets sorted so the most urgent data shows up first.
 
## Why I made it
 
I'm an LPN working in home health. My patient has a different nurse at night and we don't usually see each other, so report tends to get passed through the family. This could mean inaccurate or even missed clinical information. I wanted a way to leave and read report that is more accurate and efficient.
 
## What it does
 
You log in, select your region, then select your patient. You can only see and give report for the patients you're assigned to.
 
Report covers things that you want the next shift to know quickly without having to sift through pages of nursing notes. It includes things like vent and tube feed tolerance, PRN medications given, medication refills needed, I&Os, out-of-range vitals, skin issues, DME problems, new orders, things to pass on to the doctor, ADLs, and incidents. Items are color-coded by urgency so you can scan it quickly and prioritize tasks.
